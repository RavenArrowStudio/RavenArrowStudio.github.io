I focus on creating immersive and engaging experiences for the Meta Quest platform. This is the official home for my VR game projects.

Current Project: Thythm Boxing: Padwork

Rhythm Boxing: Padwork is an immersive VR boxing trainer that puts you face-to-face with multiple iconic fighting styles. No opponents. No damage bars. Just you, the pads, and the pure art of combination punching. Train Like the Legends Each style is a complete workout experience: Speed Demon — Lightning-fast combinations that test your reflexes and hand speed. Iron Fist — Devastating power punches that demand proper weight transfer and hip rotation. Relentless Force — Pressure fighting with non-stop volume and aggressive forward movement. Rhythm-Based Padwork Every combination follows a distinct rhythm. Land your punches on beat, and you'll unlock higher scores, longer combos, and a deeper connection to the flow of each style. Miss the rhythm? The pads will reset. Your form breaks. You start again. What You'll Get Multiple unique style modules — each with 20+ signature combination drills Full-body workout — engage your core, shoulders, and legs with proper form Endless replayability — Repeat. Refine. Become instinct. No experience needed. No gym required. Just put on your headset and find your rhythm.
