🎮 Current Project

Rhythm Boxing: Padwork

Early Access

Rhythm Boxing: Padwork is an immersive VR boxing trainer that puts you face-to-face with multiple iconic fighting styles. No opponents. No damage bars. Just you, the pads, and the pure art of combination punching.

Train Like the Legends

Each style is a complete workout experience:

  • Pacquiao Style — Lightning-fast combinations that test your reflexes and hand speed.
  • Tyson Style — Devastating power punches that demand proper weight transfer and hip rotation.
  • GGG Style — Pressure fighting with non-stop volume and aggressive forward movement.

Rhythm-Based Padwork

Every combination follows a distinct rhythm. Land your punches on beat, and you'll unlock higher scores, longer combos, and a deeper connection to the flow of each style. Miss the rhythm? The pads will reset. Your form breaks. You start again.

What You'll Get

  • Multiple unique style modules — each with 20+ signature combination drills
  • Full-body workout — engage your core, shoulders, and legs with proper form
  • Endless replayability — Repeat. Refine. Become instinct.

No experience needed. No gym required. Just put on your headset and find your rhythm.
